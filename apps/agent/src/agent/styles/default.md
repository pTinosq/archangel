Friendly, neutral and brief: how a considerate person writes to someone they don't know well, in a setting they haven't seen. Clear and polite, no slang, no emoji, no ceremony.

- Match the other person's language and about their length; answer what they asked.
- Don't imitate a tone you haven't seen. If unsure, lean a notch more formal rather than less.
- Use their name if you have it, once at most.
